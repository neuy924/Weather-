    Score bar = comfort 0–100. The Today row uses the hourly value for this hour, so it is like-for-like with past years and may differ slightly from the live reading above.

    How the comparison works
    • Versus 10 years: it pools every hour within ±7 days and ±1 hour of now, across the last 10 years (about 450 hours). A straight-line fit removes the normal seasonal slope and time-of-day slope, so the pool is centred on this exact date and hour. Today is then ranked in that pool and expressed as a difference from the average plus a standard-deviation score. Within ±0.5 SD is "about normal", 0.5–1 "slightly", 1–2 "warmer/colder", over 2 "unusually".
    • Model offset: today comes from forecast models, history from ERA5 reanalysis. Over the days where both exist (ERA5 lags about 5 days) the app measures the gap and adjusts today's value by it.
    • Versus last week: same hour on days 5–10 ago, from the same forecast model as today. The change is ranked against week-on-week changes over the last 10 years at this date, so the usual seasonal drift is accounted for.
    • Comfort: starts at 100. Feels-like loses 4 points per °C below 18°C and 6 per °C above 24°C. Rain costs 10–25, strong wind (over 25 km/h) up to 15, daytime cloud up to 8. It is ranked in the same 10-year pool. The weights are a judgement, not a standard.
    • Limits: neighbouring days are not independent, so the pool behaves like fewer than 450 hours. Ten years is a short baseline and a recent one: it shows what is normal lately, not against older climate. One hour can still be moved by a front arriving early or late.
