Feels-like at this date and hour

    Score bar = comfort 0–100. The Today row uses the hourly value for this hour, so it is like-for-like with past years and may differ slightly from the live reading above.

    How the comparison works
    • For each of the previous 5 years, it takes the same calendar date and the same local hour at your location.
    • Comfort score starts at 100. Feels-like loses 4 points per °C below 18°C and 6 per °C above 24°C. Rain at this hour costs 10–25 points, strong wind (over 25 km/h) up to 15, and daytime cloud up to 8.
    • Today is compared with the 5-year average score: within 5 points is "about typical", 5–15 "better/worse", over 15 "much".
    • Limits: five single-hour snapshots are a small, noisy sample, so a verdict says how today compares with those five days, not the climate. Today comes from forecast models and the past from ERA5 reanalysis, so small differences (about 1°C) may be model offset rather than real change. The score weights are a judgement, not a standard.
